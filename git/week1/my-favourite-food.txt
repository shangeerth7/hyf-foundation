Chicken Fried Rice Recipe

Ingredients:
- 2 cups cooked rice
- 200g chicken
- 1 egg
- 1 carrot
- 1/2 onion
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon cooking oil
- Salt and pepper

Instructions:
1. Cut the chicken, carrot, and onion into small pieces.
2. Heat the oil in a pan.
3. Cook the chicken until it is fully cooked.
4. Add the onion and carrot and cook for a few minutes.
5. Add the egg and scramble it.
6. Add the cooked rice and mix everything together.
7. Add soy sauce, salt, and pepper.
8. Stir-fry for a few minutes and serve.
