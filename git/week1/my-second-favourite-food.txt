Pasta Recipe

Ingredients:
- 200g pasta
- 1 tomato
- 1/2 onion
- 2 tablespoons tomato sauce
- 1 tablespoon cooking oil
- Salt and pepper

Instructions:
1. Boil the pasta in water until it is cooked.
2. Chop the tomato and onion.
3. Heat the oil in a pan.
4. Add the onion and tomato and cook for a few minutes.
5. Add the tomato sauce, salt, and pepper.
6. Add the cooked pasta and mix well.
7. Cook for a few more minutes and serve.
